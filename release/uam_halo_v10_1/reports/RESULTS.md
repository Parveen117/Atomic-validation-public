## Interpretation

The strict V10.1 analysis reproducibly identifies unusual local mass-surface
behaviour for a subset of externally annotated halo candidates. However, the
strength and direction of candidate-level effects depend on control-pool
construction.

The result supports a publication-grade methodology and sensitivity study. It
does not establish universal halo discrimination or direct spatial halo
structure.
