# C04 library document-delivery metadata

Use the following bibliographic record for an interlibrary-loan or document-delivery request.

```text
Article title: Simultaneous measurement of the anomalous heat capacity and resistivity of chromium near TN
Authors: M. B. Salamon; D. S. Simons; P. R. Garnier
Journal: Solid State Communications
Volume: 7
Issue: 15
Date: 1 August 1969
Pages: 1035-1038
DOI: 10.1016/0038-1098(69)90464-5
PII: 0038109869904645
Publisher: Elsevier
ISSN: 0038-1098
```

## Scan requirements

Request a copyright-compliant personal research copy including:

- every article page;
- figures at the highest available scan resolution;
- figure captions and axis labels without cropping;
- grayscale or colour preserved as held;
- no OCR-only substitute;
- no image compression beyond the library’s normal delivery process.

## Route notes

- University of Illinois affiliates can request journal articles through the University’s ILLiad/DocExpress services.
- A non-affiliated individual should use their home institution or local public library’s interlibrary-loan service.
- The received file must be preserved byte-for-byte and hashed before any image extraction or digitization.

## Intake note

Do not rename or optimize the received PDF before the original-byte SHA-256 hash is recorded. Any later cropped figure or converted image must carry its own derivative hash and a link to the original file record.
