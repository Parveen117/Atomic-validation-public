# C04 University Archives scan request draft

**Recipient:** University of Illinois Archives (`illiarch@illinois.edu`)

**Suggested subject:** Request for ARPA SD-131 chromium experiment records, 1968–1970

Dear University Archives staff,

I am seeking archival material related to a University of Illinois Materials Research Laboratory experiment published as:

> M. B. Salamon, D. S. Simons, and P. R. Garnier, “Simultaneous measurement of the anomalous heat capacity and resistivity of chromium near TN,” *Solid State Communications* 7(15), 1035–1038 (1969), DOI: 10.1016/0038-1098(69)90464-5.

The article states that the work was supported in part by Advanced Research Projects Agency Contract SD-131. The University Archives catalogue lists the collection **Materials Sciences Annual Technical Reports, 1963–**, series **11/14/818**, containing annual technical reports submitted to ARPA under SD-131.

Could you please check the 1968–69 and 1969–70 reports, or nearby files, for any of the following?

- a project entry naming Salamon, Simons, Garnier, chromium, heat capacity, resistivity, or the Néel transition;
- a preprint or technical-report version of the article;
- figures corresponding to the simultaneous heat-capacity and `dρ/dT` measurements;
- a technical-report number, data appendix, apparatus description, or related laboratory record.

A high-resolution scan of relevant pages would be sufficient. If the collection does not contain the article or figures, a brief negative confirmation or referral to another University record series would also be valuable.

The request is for scholarly, non-commercial reproducibility research. I do not intend to redistribute a complete copyrighted article.

Thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,

Monty Dabas
