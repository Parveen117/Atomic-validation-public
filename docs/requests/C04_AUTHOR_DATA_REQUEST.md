# C04 author-data request draft

**Recipient:** Myron B. Salamon (`salamon@utdallas.edu`)

**Suggested subject:** Request for 1969 chromium heat-capacity and resistivity data

Dear Professor Salamon,

I am conducting a reproducibility-focused study of the chromium Néel-transition experiment reported in:

> M. B. Salamon, D. S. Simons, and P. R. Garnier, “Simultaneous measurement of the anomalous heat capacity and resistivity of chromium near TN,” *Solid State Communications* 7(15), 1035–1038 (1969), DOI: 10.1016/0038-1098(69)90464-5.

The study is currently limited to the publisher metadata and abstract. I have not been able to obtain a primary scan containing the figures or machine-readable values for the simultaneously measured heat capacity and temperature coefficient of resistivity.

Would you be willing to share any of the following materials that may still be available?

1. an author PDF, scan, or preprint of the article containing the original figures;
2. numerical values, notebooks, tables, or reconstructed arrays for heat capacity and `dρ/dT` near the transition;
3. the figure and axis definitions, including units and temperature calibration;
4. the AC modulation frequency, amplitude, phase convention, and thermal-lag treatment;
5. specimen purity, geometry, treatment, and thermometer-calibration details;
6. pointwise uncertainty or shared calibration uncertainty for the two simultaneous channels.

A scan alone would already be valuable. I would use the material only for scholarly, non-commercial numerical reconstruction and reproducibility analysis. The original article and authorship would remain fully cited, and no full copyrighted article would be redistributed without permission.

Thank you for considering the request.

Sincerely,

Monty Dabas
