# Transparency and Inclusion Policy

This repository is intended to provide a complete, auditable publication record for the atomic-model validation programme.

## Included

The public validation archive will include all scientifically relevant material needed to understand, reproduce, challenge, or extend the reported results:

- validation code for V1 through V4;
- all tests used to verify predictors, guards, hashes, and leakage resistance;
- data provenance and processing instructions;
- exact configurations and originating commit identifiers;
- full metrics, residuals, abstentions, outliers, and failure cases;
- negative results, superseded validation stages, and diagnostic studies;
- the complete trials and runs ledger;
- claim boundaries, limitations, and known unresolved issues;
- frozen publication artefacts for the principal release.

## Excluded

Only material that is not scientifically relevant to the validation record will be excluded, including:

- credentials, tokens, private keys, and personal information;
- operating-system caches and editor files;
- duplicated generated files whose canonical hashes are already retained;
- unrelated experiments and repositories;
- temporary scratch files that do not support a reported result.

Exclusion is based on scientific relevance, security, and reproducibility, not on whether a result is favourable.

## Negative and null results

Failed approaches, outlier regimes, catastrophic residuals, and abstention cases will be retained and described. A result will not be removed because it weakens a headline metric.

## Claim discipline

The repository distinguishes among:

1. directly demonstrated computational results;
2. supported empirical interpretations;
3. broader theoretical hypotheses.

The existence of the third category does not upgrade it into the first. Reality remains annoyingly resistant to enthusiasm.
